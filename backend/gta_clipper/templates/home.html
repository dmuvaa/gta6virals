Features

Stop scrubbing. Start posting.

Long streams are full of great moments. Finding them by hand takes hours. {{ brand }} does the finding, and you do the picking.

Whole streams, in minutes

We scan the full audio over many parallel connections and only download the moments you want in HD. Even 24-hour marathons.

Finds the action, not just noise

Reactions, screams, chases and shootouts all score. Every moment grows to fit the action, from 15 to 45 seconds with a short build-up.

Review at keyboard speed

Previews load instantly. Press K to keep, X to discard, nudge the start and end, and move on. Hundreds of moments in one sitting.

Webcam found automatically

The 9:16 layout puts the streamer's face cam on top and gameplay below. We find the webcam box for you, or you can draw it.

Captions that pop

Bold, word-by-word captions with the spoken word highlighted, transcribed automatically and burned into the clip.

Skips the dead air

Menus, loading screens, "be right back" and just-chatting segments get flagged automatically, so you spend your time on gameplay.

How it works

From VOD to posted in three steps

Paste a link

Any YouTube video or stream replay, or any Twitch VOD or clip. Pick your layout, and turn on captions if you want them.

Review the moments

We line up every moment with action, best first. Play, keep or discard, and trim. Flagged non-gameplay is hidden for you.

Download & post

Kept clips are made in HD in seconds. Download them one by one or all at once, then post to TikTok, Shorts, Reels and X.
