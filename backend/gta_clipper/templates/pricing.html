Billing questions

What counts as an hour of video?

The length of video we scan. A full 12-hour stream uses 12 hours. Scan just 9:00:00–11:00:00 of it and it uses 2. Unused hours don't roll over.

What counts as an export?

Each moment you keep and we make in HD. Re-making a clip you already kept (new trim, layout or captions) is free.
