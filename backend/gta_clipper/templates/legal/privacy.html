Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Draft for review: have a lawyer check this policy against the laws where your users live (for example GDPR and CCPA) before launch.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email, name (optional) and a hashed version of your password. We never store your password itself.
  • Projects: the video links you submit, plus the previews and clips made from them, stored temporarily.
  • Usage: how many hours you've scanned and clips you've exported each month, to apply plan limits.
  • Billing: if you subscribe, Paddle (our merchant of record) handles your payment details and billing address. We only receive a customer ID and your subscription status, never your card number.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

To run the service, apply plan limits, manage subscriptions, keep accounts secure, and answer support requests. We don't sell your personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when fonts are fetched.

Who we share it with

  • Paddle, which sells subscriptions on our behalf and processes payments.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs our servers and stores data on our behalf.
  • Video platforms (such as YouTube and Twitch), which receive requests from our servers when we fetch videos you link to.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Projects, previews and clips are deleted automatically after a short period, usually 48 hours. Account and billing records are kept while your account is open and as long as the law requires afterwards.

Your choices

You can delete any project, or your whole account and all its projects, from your account page. To get a copy of your data or ask a question, email {{ support_email }}.

Changes

If we make significant changes, we'll update this page and tell you by email or in the app.
